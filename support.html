Support

Stuck, found a bug or have an idea? Email support@dumpthatpic.com and we’ll get back to you.

Where do dumped photos go?

To your Recently Deleted album in Photos, where they stay for 30 days, so nothing is gone for good straight away. To free the space now: open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All.

I dumped a photo by mistake

Tap Undo straight away while swiping. If you’ve already confirmed, open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted, select the photo and tap Recover.

The app can only see some of my photos

You gave it limited access. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Dump That Pic and choose Full Access.

Do you upload my photos?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See our privacy policy.

How do I start again?

In the app, open Settings → Reset all progress. Your photos aren’t touched.

How does the free trial work?

Tap any PRO feature and choose Start 7-day free trial. You get every Pro feature for 7 days. No payment details are needed and nothing is charged when it ends. After that, swiping month by month stays free, and Pro is a single one-time purchase. No subscription.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then open Settings → Restore Purchases in the app.

What are sample photos?

Settings → Use sample photos swaps your library for a set of sample photos, so you can try the app or film it without showing your own pictures. Your real photos and progress aren’t touched. Turn it off to go back.
